Help
  1. Open the ChatGPT conversation you want to coordinate from. Open this popup and select Connect & watch to connect that chat.
  2. For Grok, open its conversation in another tab, open this popup and select Pair Grok. For Gemini, open gemini.google.com/app and select Pair Gemini, or use its slim Pair bar here. Keep paired conversation tabs open.
  3. Copy the instructions below, paste them into your new or existing ChatGPT conversation and send them. Then ask ChatGPT for your task. Grok receives its reply rules automatically with each new relayed task.

Click the Codex/Grok card or Gemini bar to open Details. Click completed or failed totals to see matching jobs across all agents; select a row to inspect its details. Filters cover the latest 100 retained agent jobs, so old totals may exceed the displayed history. Open Job plan and click a job to read its full prompt. Queued jobs can be removed or moved up/down; running and finished jobs are protected. Stale edits are rejected if the plan changed. Codex tokens come from the updated local bridge; cached input is included in input. Grok and Gemini website tokens and monetary charges are not reported.

Using the popup

Relay shows live agent states, queue counts and results. The Jobs tab lists planned and agent jobs by title across Codex, Grok, Gemini and ChatGPT. Click a title for instructions and results, or filter by queued/working/passed/failed. Saved snapshots are clearly labelled when live status is unavailable. Queued plan jobs can be moved or removed there. Open Model & thinking for quick settings. Advanced has separate Testing and Diagnostics tabs. Testing holds connection checks, agent tests and automatic setup. Diagnostics explains the current state and shows connections, listener checkpoints, completion evidence, drafts, delivery, agent queues and activity. Connection & execution settings and technical details can be expanded there. A green toolbar icon means work is active; blue means listening, grey means idle, amber means waiting or reconnecting, and red means an error.

If a new chat is not receiving tasks, select it and use Connect & watch. Pairing Grok does not replace the connected ChatGPT chat. Leave both tabs open. Clearing a draft can release a waiting result. Reload the add-on and refresh both chats after an upgrade.

Saved outputs and alerts

Jobs → Outputs opens the shared local library. Replies, code blocks, readable images and supported downloads are saved automatically. Select up to four outputs, choose Use in job, pick Codex, Grok or Gemini and enter instructions. Text/code can be included in full or attached as a file. Queued jobs keep their own copies when library items are deleted. The library holds 500 MB until you delete items; when full, saving pauses while jobs and result delivery continue. Old retained text is backfilled; previously released images/files are marked unavailable. For Codex deliverables, ask it to list created or modified final file paths in its structured artifacts array. Desktop alerts are configured in Advanced and need Enable alerts permission.
